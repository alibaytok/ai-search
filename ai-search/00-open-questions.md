## Rules

- Every item has: ID, Status, Related Phase, Rationale.
- IDs are stable and never reused.
- Status values:
  - OPEN: unresolved, requires Codex action.
  - IN-REVIEW: proposed answer pending Codex decision.
  - RESOLVED: closed by recorded decision (link to decision ID).
  - DEFERRED: intentionally postponed to a later phase by Codex.
  - WITHDRAWN: no longer relevant; rationale required.
- Claude must not move an item to RESOLVED without a corresponding decision authored by Codex.
- Unknowns are never silently dropped. If something becomes irrelevant, mark WITHDRAWN with rationale.
